Invariant Network

A small, invite-only initiative focused on matching a limited number of rigorously screened quantitative and applied machine learning profiles with teams hiring for depth rather than volume.

This is not a job portal, resume database, or mass recruitment platform. There are no public listings, no bulk applications, and no automated matching.

Who this is for

  • Teams hiring for quantitative, modeling-heavy, or applied ML roles
  • Employers frustrated with noise from traditional job portals
  • Candidates looking for serious roles aligned with their depth and background

How it works

  • Candidates share profiles for manual screening
  • Employers share role context privately (no public posting)
  • Introductions are made selectively when there is a strong fit
  • No profile or role is shared without explicit consent

What this is not

  • No open applications
  • No recruiter spam
  • No resume circulation
  • No guaranteed placements

This is an independent, private initiative and is not affiliated with any institution or employer.
